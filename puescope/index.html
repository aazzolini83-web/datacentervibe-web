// VIBE CODING PROMPT SUITE

Build what
comes next.

Keep the essential energy-data prompts close at hand. Convert meter exports, define boundaries, validate quality and prepare the model for governed online operation.

PROMPT 01Map my meter registryStructure meter identity, role and hierarchy
Transform the attached source into PUEScope METERS. Preserve Meter_ID, serial and IP. Normalize Meter_Role to TOTAL_FACILITY, IT_ENERGY, COOLING, ELECTRICAL_LOSSES, LIGHTING, AUXILIARY, RENEWABLE_GENERATION or OTHER. Do not invent data. Mark ambiguity with REVIEW in Notes.
PROMPT 02Transform energy readingsPreserve periods, kWh and data quality
Transform the attached energy export into PUEScope READINGS. Do not invent readings. Never convert kW to kWh without an explicit duration. Preserve periods, units and quality. Mark estimates as ESTIMATED and unresolved records as REVIEW.
PROMPT 03Define the PUE boundaryAvoid parent-child double counting
Propose a PUEScope BOUNDARIES mapping from the attached meter hierarchy. Do not select parent and child meters that represent the same energy. Separate TOTAL_FACILITY, IT_ENERGY and COOLING. Return assumptions separately.
PROMPT 04Review energy data qualityDetect duplicates, gaps and invalid relationships
Review the PUEScope workbook for duplicate Meter_ID values, mixed units, overlapping periods, missing boundary readings, parent-child double counting and IT energy greater than facility energy. Do not change data.
