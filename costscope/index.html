// VIBE CODING PROMPT SUITE

Build what
comes next.

Keep the essential financial-data prompts close at hand. Normalize budgets, separate forecast from committed and actual costs, connect capacity, and validate the economics model without inventing amounts.

PROMPT 01Map my approved budgetsNormalize CAPEX, OPEX, categories and scope
Transform the attached source into CostScope BUDGETS. Preserve approved amounts, references, currency, owner and year. Normalize Cost_Type to CAPEX or OPEX. Normalize Cost_Category and Scope to the CostScope controlled values. Do not invent amounts, owners or references. Mark ambiguity with REVIEW in Comments.
PROMPT 02Transform cost entriesKeep Forecast, Committed and Actual separate
Transform the attached source into CostScope COSTS. Preserve Forecast, Committed and Actual as separate fields. Do not interpret a contingency reserve as Actual cost. Keep supplier, reference, period, currency and status. Do not invent values. Mark unresolved classification with REVIEW in Comments.
PROMPT 03Connect capacity snapshotsPrepare unit economics without invented capacity
Transform the attached capacity source into CostScope CAPACITY with Period, Site, Racks, Installed_U, Occupied_U and Average_IT_kW. Preserve the source values. Do not estimate missing capacity. Flag Occupied_U greater than Installed_U and mark unresolved fields for review.
PROMPT 04Review economics data qualityDetect duplicates, invalid categories and broken links
Review the CostScope workbook for duplicate IDs, invalid CAPEX or OPEX values, uncontrolled categories or scopes, negative amounts, missing budget links, inconsistent periods and Occupied_U greater than Installed_U. Do not modify the data. Return an issue table with severity and recommended correction.
