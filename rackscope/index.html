How It Works

  1. Fill the Excel template: Define device names, U heights, and power specs.
  2. Load into RackScope: Open the HTML file in any browser and upload your spreadsheet.
  3. Visual Analysis: Render rack layouts, check space availability, and monitor power distribution instantly.
